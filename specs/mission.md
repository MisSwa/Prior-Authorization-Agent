# Mission

## Purpose

To streamline and accelerate the prior authorization process in healthcare by providing clinicians and administrative staff with an AI-assisted tool that evaluates patient cases against payer policies — delivering faster, more consistent, and auditable coverage decisions.

## Problem Statement

Prior authorization is one of the most burdensome administrative processes in modern healthcare. Clinicians spend hours gathering clinical evidence, cross-referencing payer criteria, and drafting justification letters — time taken away from patient care. Errors and inconsistencies in submissions lead to denials, appeals, and delays that directly harm patients awaiting necessary treatment.

## Mission Statement

Empower healthcare teams with an intelligent prior authorization assistant that reads clinical documentation, evaluates coverage criteria, and produces clear, compliant recommendations — so every authorization decision is faster, traceable, and grounded in policy.

## Core Values

### Speed
Reduce the time from clinical documentation to authorization recommendation from hours to minutes. Every minute saved is a minute returned to patient care.

### Accuracy
Ground every recommendation in the actual text of the payer policy. Surface the specific criteria met or unmet. Never guess; always cite.

### Compliance & Auditability
Every decision must be explainable. Maintain a full audit trail — which documents were reviewed, which criteria were evaluated, what the model concluded, and why — so teams can defend every recommendation to payers and regulators.

## Who We Serve

- **Clinicians** who need fast, evidence-backed justifications for treatment plans
- **Authorization coordinators** who manage submission volume and track payer requirements
- **Compliance officers** who need auditable records of every decision
- **Healthcare administrators** who need visibility into denial patterns and approval rates

## What This System Does Not Do

- Make final authorization decisions — a human coordinator always approves
- Submit directly to payers
- Access live EHR systems
- Replace clinical judgment

## What Success Looks Like

- A clinician uploads a patient chart and a payer policy, and within seconds receives a structured prior authorization recommendation with cited criteria
- Every recommendation includes a traceable rationale that can be copied directly into a payer submission
- Coordinators can review the full decision audit trail — inputs, agent reasoning steps, and final output — for any case
- Denial rates decrease because submissions are better aligned with payer criteria before they are sent
